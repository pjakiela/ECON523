# Pre-Analysis Plan Project

_Due May 19, 2026, at 5:00 PM_

<br>

The objective for this project is to produce a pre-analysis plan for a proposed randomized evaluation of a development intervention.  

<br>

### Important Dates and Deadlines

**By Friday, April 10:** submit the gradescope form indicating your preferred topic area and geographic focus. This will include 1-2 paragraphs describing specific policy interventions that you might be interested in evaluating.  

**By Friday, April 24:** submit 1-2 page document describing the intervention that you propose to analyze and summarizing the results of 2-3 relevant RCTs that motivate the design of your study.

**Week of April 27:** meetings with Prof. Jakiela to discuss your proposed design

**By Friday, May 8:** initial draft of the pre-analysis plan

**By Tuesday, May 19:** final drafts due.
