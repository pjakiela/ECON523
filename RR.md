# Pre-Analysis Plan Project

_Due May 18, 2025, at 11:00 PM_

The objective for this project is to produce a pre-analysis plan for a proposed randomized evaluation of a development intervention. The pre-analysis plan will follow the structure provided by the _Journal of Development Economics_, which accepts not-yet-completed papers as Registered Reports based on the pre-analysis plan.  

The _Journal of Development Economics_ Registered Report submission template is available [here](Stage_1_Submission_Template.pdf). You should follow the guidelines provide there when structuring your report, with the following exceptions: 
1. Omit the reference to **study pre-registration**
2. Omit the **reporting checklist**
3. You do not need to discuss **multiple hypothesis testing**, though you are welcome to do so if you wish
4. Omit the section on **administrative information**

Text within each of the sections of your pre-analysis plan should be structured as it would appear in a paper, in prose form, and not as bullet-point answers to the individual questions. All the text must be written by you, and I may ask you to come discuss the papers you cite or the details of your proposed research design before assigning your grade.  

Additional information on Registered Reports is available [here](https://afosterri.org/jdepreresults/author-guidelines/). An example of a Registered Report that was accepted by the _Journal of Development Economics_ is available [here](https://pjakiela.github.io/research/EMERGE-registered-report-accepted.pdf).  

You will submit your registered report as a pdf along with a do file that generates your power calculations.  

Though you are not required to do so, I encourage you to email me with a proposed topic for your pre-analysis plan by May 2. I will respond to proposals received by then with suggestions of relevant literature and interventions you might wish to build on.
